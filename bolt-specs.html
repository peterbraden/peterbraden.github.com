Term Meaning
Tap drill Drill this hole before cutting internal threads (75% engagement, coarse pitch)
Clearance — Fine Tight fit; bolt passes through with minimal slop. Use when alignment matters.
Clearance — Medium General-purpose through-hole. Most common choice.
Clearance — Coarse Loose fit; easier assembly, tolerates misalignment.
